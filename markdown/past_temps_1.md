### Winter Conditions During Past El Niños

#### Temperature

The maps to the right illustrate the average temperature departures for winter during all El Niño years since 1950.

Cooler-than-normal temperatures, shown by the blue colors, seem to be more prominent during El Niño winters between 1950 and 1980 (the first 12 maps). However, for El Niño winters since 1982 (the last 15 maps), orange and red colors, indicating warmer-than-normal temperatures, appear more frequently.

During strong El Niño winters, temperatures were generally warmer than normal, particularly during the very strong events in 1997-98 and 2015-16. Meanwhile, temperatures tended to lean cooler than normal during weak El Niño winters including 1979-80 and 2014-15.
