[NOAA’s High Tide Flooding Outlook](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/high-tide-flooding/annual-outlook.html) states that the Mid-Atlantic is predicted to see 15 to 20 high tide flooding days between October 2026 and April 2027. The outlook notes, “More than 75% of stations in the region are predicted to meet or exceed their high tide flood day records as [El Niño](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/monitoring/enso/) is expected to compound the impact of sea level change.”
