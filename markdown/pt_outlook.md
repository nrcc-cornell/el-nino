### Winter Temperature and Precipitation Outlooks

As of mid-August, [NOAA's winter outlooks](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/) (right) show that above-normal temperatures are favored for the entire Northeast, with the greatest likelihood in northern Pennsylvania, most of New York, northern New England, much of Massachusetts, and northwestern Connecticut.

The precipitation outlook indicates that much of the Northeast could see above-normal precipitation due to a favored storm track across the southern U.S. and up the East Coast. Part of southern Maryland and the southern end of the Delmarva Peninsula have the greatest likelihood of above-normal precipitation. Equal chances of below-, near-, or above-normal precipitation were predicted in some interior and northern areas where there were no clear signals to sway the forecast, such as western West Virginia, northwestern Pennsylvania, western and northern New York, and much of Maine. Additionally, lake-effect snowfall tends to be reduced in parts of New York during El Niño events.
