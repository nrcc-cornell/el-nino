### El Niño Strength

El Niño conditions were present and strengthening in the equatorial Pacific Ocean [during August](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso_advisory/ensodisc.shtml). NOAA’s Climate Prediction Center expects this El Niño to become very strong, noting that “there is a 75% chance of a historic event that would exceed the strength of previous El Niño events dating back to 1950.” Other very strong El Niño years included 1965-66, 1972-73, 1982-83, 1991-92, 1997-98, and 2015-16

The outlook indicates a [100% chance](https://cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso/roni/probabilities/) of El Niño conditions during fall 2026 and winter 2026-27, with an 82% chance of El Niño during spring 2027. A very strong event is favored through winter ([75% to 98% chance](https://cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso/roni/strengths/)), with weakening expected during spring. With such a strong event, there is a higher likelihood of seeing “typical” El Niño weather patterns and impacts.
